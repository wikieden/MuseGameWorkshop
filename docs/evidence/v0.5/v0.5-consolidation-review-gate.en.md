# v0.5 Roadmap Rebase & Research Consolidation Review Gate

Review Status: Approved

Date: 2026-06-10

Language: English

## 1. Approved Scope

This review gate approves v0.5 as the Roadmap Rebase & Research Consolidation version.

Allowed scope:

- Close the v0.5b AI art dry-run evidence.
- Land the Engine AI Development Adapters overview.
- Land independent Godot / Unreal AI tool research reports.
- Land the user feedback scan.
- Record the Godot integration decision: Godot AI first, GodotIQ as the second-layer reserve, and GodotPrompter as an internal skill-design reference.
- Rebase versioning from `v0.10 -> v1.0 / v1.1+` to `v0.10 -> v0.11 -> v0.12 -> v0.13 -> v0.14 -> v1.0`.
- Add v0.5 consolidation evidence and a check entry point.

## 2. Explicitly Not Approved

This review gate does not approve:

- Installing or integrating Godot AI, GodotIQ, GodotPrompter, MCP servers, or any external tool.
- Creating new Godot / Unreal engine files.
- Generating new AI art, animation, music, SFX, or UI assets.
- Importing final or release-candidate assets.
- Integrating commercial creative-tool APIs.
- Automatic publishing, store-page publishing, account systems, or LiveOps implementation.

## 3. Completion Conditions

v0.5 is complete only when:

- The v0.5 AI art dry-run check passes.
- The Engine AI Development Adapters overview exists in Chinese and English.
- Seven independent research reports exist in Chinese and English pairs.
- The user feedback scan exists in Chinese and English.
- The Godot integration decision is written into the research overview.
- Roadmap, delivery plan, overall plan, and README agree on the v0.5-v1.0 version order.
- `check-v0.5-consolidation` passes.

## 4. Next Stage

After v0.5, the next stage is v0.6 Game Design & Systems.

v0.6 should not directly install Godot AI or integrate MCP. A Godot AI Adapter Dry Run requires its own review gate.
