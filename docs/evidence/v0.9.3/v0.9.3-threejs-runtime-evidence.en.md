## Production Claims

- Three.js WebGPU is a supported final production direction.
- WebGL 2 remains a required fallback until a reviewed project browser matrix permits its removal.
- Unit, build, browser automation, and manual browser playtest are separate evidence layers.
- Installed dependencies or configured browsers that fail validation are blocking failures, not skipped checks.

## Boundary Evidence

- v0.9.3 does not approve a commercial backend, payment, monetization, LiveOps, or real telemetry service.
- The browser reference is a validation slice, not final art, release balance, or a release build.
- Unity, Unreal Engine, and Godot implementation claims require their own target-specific evidence.
